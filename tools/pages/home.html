Why it exists

Group study is broken in three ways.

1

Studying alone is boring.

So you don't, until the night before.

2

Group study turns into chatting.

Ten memes later, nobody has opened the slides.

3

Nobody knows what the group doesn't understand.

Everyone nods. Everyone is lost on the same thing.

Cramberry makes the group chat itself the study session.

Someone says "im so cooked on caches", and two minutes later the whole squad is fighting the Caches Boss together. Every right answer drains its HP; every wrong one lets it hit back. When the fight ends, the squad sees its weakest topic, and each student's Cubbie quietly texts them what they missed.

    The games

    Four ways to study without it feeling like studying.

    HA HA HA!

    ⚔️ Boss Battle

    /battle <topic>

    Everyone answers A–D. Right answers damage the boss; wrong ones let it hit the squad back. 3 in a row is a ⚡ critical hit, one 🎲 50/50 per battle, and the final strike lets you 💰 wager half or all in.

    🕵️ Two Truths & a Lie

    /lie <topic>

    Three statements, one is a plausible misconception. Find the lie before the dragonfruit liar wins.

    🎤 Explain It

    /explain <topic>

    Cramberry calls on someone to explain a concept in their own words. The AI grades it 0–10 and says what to add. Pass, and it's open floor for half XP.

    📅 Daily Duel

    /daily

    One question a day to keep your streak and the squad streak alive. /daily 8pm schedules it, /daily off stops it.

    The boss bestiary

    Each topic gets its own fruit boss, always the same one for the same topic. Below 40% HP a boss gets a bandage and one eye shut; at 0 it turns grey. Hover to hurt one, click to make it laugh.

    Type what you're cooked on. This is the bot's real rule: the topic's letters pick the fruit.
