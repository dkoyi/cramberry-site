The rules you just played

Straight from the bot's code.

  • Boss and squad start at 100 HP. Each question can deal 20 HP × the share of the squad that got it right.
  • A right answer on a 3-in-a-row streak is a ⚡ crit: +50 points, and the question hits 1.5× harder.
  • Points: 100 for a right answer, plus 50 / 30 / 10 for the first three right answers.
  • Any wrong answers let the boss hit back for 35 × the wrong share. Nobody answers: −10.
  • One 🎲 50/50 per battle. Before the last question, 💰 wager half or all in.
  • Boss at or under 30 HP after the last question is a win. Squad at 0 HP is a wipe.
